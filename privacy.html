Privacy Policy — VYCE6 Publisher

Last updated: November 13, 2026

What this app is

VYCE6 Publisher is a private tool operated by the owner of the VYCE6 channels. It uploads videos produced by VYCE6 to VYCE6's own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts, schedules them, and reads the statistics of those videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): the ability to upload and update videos on the VYCE6 channels, and the analytics of those channels (views, watch time, audience retention, subscribers).
  • TikTok (via TikTok's Content Posting and Display APIs): the ability to publish videos to the VYCE6 TikTok accounts, and the public statistics of those videos.

We do not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or of any other user. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and channel statistics are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local database that is never published. Statistics are kept for as long as they are useful to evaluate the channels' performance, and tokens until access is revoked. The owner can delete all of it at any time.

Revoking access

Access granted to this app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) and from TikTok's "Manage app permissions" settings.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
